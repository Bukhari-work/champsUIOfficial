<p class="mb-6 leading-relaxed">This full-day workshop introduced Structural Equation Modeling–Partial Least Squares (SEM-PLS) using SmartPLS, combining the method’s concepts with practical analysis for evidence-informed health policy. The brief emphasizes that sound data and appropriate analytical methods can help translate research evidence into stronger health regulation and policy.</p>

## Intended participants

- Public health specialists and health practitioners.
- Health researchers and medical-records or health-information professionals.
- Staff from health offices, ministries, NGOs, and other institutions.
- Postgraduate students and prospective data-informed policy professionals.

## Analysis topics

- Measurement-model and structural-model assessment.
- Mediation analysis and core SEM-PLS concepts.
- Distribution assumptions, sample size, and statistical power.
- Goodness-of-fit assessment and interpretation.

The fee was **IDR 1,500,000**, including materials, a workshop kit, a certificate with 3 Ministry of Health credit points, lunch, and coffee breaks. Travel and accommodation were not included.
