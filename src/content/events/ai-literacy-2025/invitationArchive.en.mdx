## Learning objectives

- Understand foundational AI literacy and ethical use of health data.
- Learn how to access and process BPJS Kesehatan sample data.
- Explore approaches to writing scientific publications using secondary data.
- Strengthen data analysis and interpretation for health research.
- Connect with researchers, academics, and health practitioners.

## Intended participants

- Researchers and academics interested in data- and technology-enabled research.
- Diploma and undergraduate or postgraduate students in public health, policy, data science, and related fields.
- Health practitioners using evidence to inform decisions.
- Technology professionals interested in AI applications in health research.
